# Licenses and asset provenance

- All SVG layouts, CSS, animation definitions, and written content in this project were authored for this profile README.
- No third-party icon packs or downloaded brand marks are embedded. Technology names are rendered as text labels to avoid inaccurate or unlicensed icon artwork.
- Typography uses system font stacks (`Georgia`/serif and common monospace families); no external font files or network-hosted fonts are required.
- The portrait (`assets/id.png`) and laptop character (`assets/laptop-character.png`) are user-provided assets and are not included because they were not present in the supplied workspace. Add the original files without altering their transparency.
